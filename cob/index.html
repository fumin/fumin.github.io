Cob house

Address: 台灣台南市東山區東原村瓦厝68號

I participated in the construction of this cob house in Tainan, Taiwan. The architect and lead engineer is my friend Zayin Hung. Several unforgettable highlights of this endeavour include stirring horse manure for the fine polishing, stealth midnight excavation missions for acquisition of quality cob, and cool river bathing after work.
